How to play

Keyboard

  • ← → move
  • ↓ soft drop
  • Space hard drop
  • ↑ / X rotate right
  • Z / Ctrl rotate left
  • C / Shift hold
  • P / Esc pause

Touch & mouse

  • Drag on the board to move
  • Tap the board to rotate
  • Drag down to soft drop
  • Flick down to hard drop
  • Flick up to hold
  • Or use the on-screen pads
