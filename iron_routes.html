    How to play

    Score the most points by claiming railway routes, linking the cities on your destination tickets, and building the longest continuous line.

    On your turn, do one thing

    • Draw train cards. Take two cards, from the five face-up cards or the face-down deck. A face-up locomotive is wild, but taking one uses your whole turn, and it can't be your second card.
    • Claim a route. Tap a route on the map. Pay as many cards of its colour as it has spaces. Grey routes take any one colour. Locomotives can stand in for any colour.
    • Draw destination tickets. Draw three tickets and keep at least one.

    Scoring

    • Routes score as you claim them: 1, 3, 5, 8, 11 or 15 points for 1 to 6 spaces.
    • At the end, each ticket whose two cities you've joined with your own routes adds its value. Each unfinished ticket subtracts it.
    • The longest continuous line earns a 12-point bonus. Ties all score it.

    Details

    • Everyone starts with 40 trains, 4 train cards, and 3 tickets, and keeps at least 2 of the tickets.
    • If three locomotives are ever face up together, all five face-up cards are replaced.
    • Double routes: one player can't claim both tracks. With 2 or 3 players, only one track of each pair can be used.
    • When a player ends a turn with 2 or fewer trains, everyone, including that player, gets one last turn.

    Computer players

    • Easy computers play loosely. Normal computers plan the shortest lines for their tickets. Hard computers also take on more tickets, and grab locomotives when they're short of cards.
    • Computers' cards and tickets stay hidden. Use the speed buttons during their turns.

    Controls

    Drag to pan and pinch, use the mouse wheel, or use the + and − buttons to zoom. Tap a ticket to highlight its cities. You can also claim from the Routes you can claim list. Keyboard: D draws from the deck, 1–5 take a face-up card, T draws tickets, Esc clears a selection. The game saves on this device after every move.
