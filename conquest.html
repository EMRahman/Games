    How to play

    Conquer every territory on the map. After setup, each turn has three phases.

    Setup

    • In the standard setup, players take turns claiming one empty (grey) territory each, placing one army on it, until the whole map is claimed.
    • Players then keep taking turns, adding one army at a time to their own territories, until everyone has placed all their starting armies (46 each for 2 players, 40 for 3, 34 for 4, 29 for 5, 25 for 6).
    • You can instead have territories dealt at random, or deal and place everything at random to start playing straight away.

    1 · Reinforce

    • Receive armies equal to your territories ÷ 3 (minimum 3), plus a bonus for every region you hold completely.
    • Trade a set of three cards for extra armies: three of a kind, one of each, or any two with a wild. Sets are worth 5, 7, 9, 11, 14, 17, then 3 more each time. Holding 5 or more cards forces a trade.
    • If a traded card shows a territory you own, 3 bonus armies are placed there.
    • Tap your territories to place armies. Choose 1, 5 or all at a time.

    2 · Attack

    • Pick one of your territories with at least 2 armies, then an adjacent enemy. Dashed lines are sea lanes; Klondike and Chukotka connect across the map edge.
    • Attack with up to 3 dice (one fewer than your armies); the defender rolls up to 2. Highest dice are compared in pairs and ties go to the defender.
    • Blitz keeps rolling until you win or are down to one army.
    • When you take a territory, move in at least as many armies as dice you rolled. Eliminate a player to take their cards.

    3 · Fortify

    • Make one move between two of your territories joined by a chain of your own territories, then your turn ends.
    • Conquer at least one territory during your turn to earn a card.

    Computer opponents

    • Tap Human or CPU next to each name when setting up. You can mix both, or let the computers play each other.
    • Easy computers attack only with big advantages; Normal and Hard chase regions, cards and weak players, and Hard takes riskier attacks.
    • Change how fast computers play with the speed buttons shown during their turns. Their cards stay hidden.

    Controls

    Drag or pinch to move and zoom the map; use the mouse wheel on desktop. Keyboard: Tab to reach a territory and Enter to select it, R roll, B blitz, N next phase, U undo, Esc cancel, +/−/0 zoom. The game saves itself on this device after every move.
